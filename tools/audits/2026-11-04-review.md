# Review of 2026-11-04: what to change in Cross_Chain

The review of the release of 2026-11-03 (page 0.14.0 and the LUKSO authorization page, Cross_Chain @ `0384f03`,
with the app 0.10.0, the extension 0.1.9 and the signing service), made right after it by two models of
different providers (Claude and Codex), as the review policy says (`AUDIT.md`). Not a professional audit.
The full reports stay private until the fixes are released; then `AUDIT.md` gets the public summary.
`WALLET-BACKLOG.md` (item 8) tracks this file with those of the other repositories.

The reviewers found no path that moves funds, changes a UP's permissions or obtains an unintended signature
without the user approving it in their own wallet; the three contracts rebuild byte for byte from the
committed sources, on every chain. The new LUKSO authorization page: no logic error.

Status: **Proposed** (decision to confirm), **To do**, **Accepted**, **Done** (with the commit).
